Koel View Point

Witness the breathtaking sunset and panoramic valley views at Netarhat's iconic Koel View Point.

History of Koel View Point

Koel View Point, located in Netarhat, Jharkhand, is renowned for its mesmerizing views of the Koel River meandering through lush green valleys. This spot has been a favorite among travelers and nature lovers for decades, offering a tranquil escape and a perfect vantage point for sunrise and sunset. The viewpoint is named after the Koel River, which is a lifeline for the region and holds cultural significance for the local communities. Over the years, Koel View Point has become a symbol of Netarhat's natural beauty and serenity.
