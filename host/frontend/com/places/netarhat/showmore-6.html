Sunset Viewpoint

Witness the magical sunset at Netarhat's most iconic viewpoint

History of Sunset Viewpoint

Sunset Viewpoint in Netarhat is renowned for its breathtaking panoramic views of the sun setting behind the hills. This spot has been a favorite among travelers and nature lovers for decades. The viewpoint is surrounded by lush forests and rolling hills, making it a perfect place to unwind and connect with nature. The tradition of gathering here to watch the sunset dates back to the British era, when officers and their families would visit Netarhat for its cool climate and scenic beauty.
