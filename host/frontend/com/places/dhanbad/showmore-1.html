Maithan Dam

Discover the scenic beauty and engineering marvel of Maithan Dam in Dhanbad

History of Maithan Dam

Maithan Dam, located on the Barakar River near Dhanbad, Jharkhand, is a significant hydroelectric and irrigation project. Built in 1957, it was designed to control floods and provide water for agriculture and power generation. The dam is renowned for its picturesque reservoir, lush surroundings, and the underground power station, which is a unique feature in India. Over the years, Maithan Dam has become a popular tourist destination, attracting visitors for its natural beauty, boating, and picnic spots.
